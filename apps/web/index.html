How to play

Goal

Get Ember and Tide into the glowing exit together. Every level needs both of you - nobody can finish alone.

Elements

  • Ember is fire: safe in lava, extinguished by water.
  • Tide is water: safe in water, boiled by lava.
  • Green toxin, spikes, saws and live electricity hurt you both.

The Resonance Link

When you are close and can see each other, an energy tether joins you. Any ◆ crystal the tether passes through is powered.

Controls

Move←→ or AD

RestartR · Pause Esc
TouchOn-screen buttons appear on phones & tablets

Things you'll meet

  • Plates hold doors open while weighed down.
  • Levers toggle; nodes react only to their element.
  • Ice melts at Ember's touch. Crates can be pushed and stood on.
  • Mirrors rotate to bend light beams into receivers.
  • Fans lift you. Matching colours show what's wired together.

Scoring

Up to 3 stars per level: finish, beat the par time, and keep combined deaths to 2 or fewer. A full run takes about 30-45 minutes.
